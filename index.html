One Last Letter

No pressure. No asking you to stay. Just a thank you.

To you,

I don't really know how to put everything I feel into words right now. I still love you, and honestly, a part of me still wishes things could have turned out differently.

But I heard you. I understand that you're tired, and I respect the peace and space you asked for.

I made this little place because I didn't want our memories to disappear just because our relationship ended. What we had was real to me. The laughs, the random moments, the pictures, the days we spent together, and even the difficult parts—they all became a part of my life.

Thank you for loving me. Thank you for choosing me for the time that you did. Thank you for every memory you gave me.

I won't ask you to come back through this letter. I just want you to know that I will always be grateful that our paths crossed.

If this really is our goodbye, then goodbye, my love. I hope life becomes kinder to you. I hope you find the peace you're looking for. And wherever life takes us, I'll always be thankful that, for a while, it was us.

Take care of yourself.

— Riddick ♡
